## 3. The Account and the Balance

### 3.1 Nature of the displayed balance

The balance displayed in the app is an accounting record of credit (a pending settlement in your favor), and does not constitute a bank deposit, a securities account, or an insured financial product. The funds corresponding to that balance are processed and held by TipPal through its payment processor (Stripe) under TipPal's processing credentials, not under an individual user account, until the applicable verification requirements are completed.

### 3.2 Processing model (Payment Facilitator / Master Merchant – Sub-merchant)

TipPal operates under a payment platform model (Payment Facilitator), analogous to a mobile point-of-sale device assigned by an employer to a salesperson: the user processes charges using TipPal's processing credential, not a bank account of their own. Under this model:

1. The user expressly authorizes TipPal to process charges on their behalf, without this making TipPal the ultimate owner of the funds.
2. The displayed balance is a pending settlement, not a deposit.
3. Withdrawal (cash out) is subject to the identity verification (KYC) required by applicable regulation and by the payment processor.
4. TipPal is not a financial institution; it is a technology platform for payment processing that operates together with a registered payment processor (Stripe).
5. TipPal maintains sufficient operating reserves to support the availability of funds corresponding to authorized withdrawals.

### 3.3 Identity verification (KYC) and holding of funds

In order to withdraw funds ("Cash Out"), the user must complete the identity verification (KYC) process required by applicable law, including anti-money laundering (AML) regulations under the Bank Secrecy Act (31 U.S.C. § 5311 et seq.) and its implementing regulations (31 C.F.R. Chapter X). TipPal may withhold, delay, suspend, or reject withdrawal requests while KYC is incomplete or during compliance reviews, without this giving rise to any liability of TipPal toward the user.

### 3.4 Internal credit transfers

Users may transfer their available balance to other registered users within the app. These transfers constitute internal accounting adjustments and not new external financial transactions. KYC is not required for internal transfers as long as neither party requests a withdrawal to an external bank account or card.

### 3.5 No interest

TipPal does not pay interest on balances held on the platform.

### 3.6 Use of funds in custody

Funds corresponding to balances that have not been withdrawn may be held in TipPal's operating accounts while in pending-settlement status, and do not generate any yield or interest for the user.
